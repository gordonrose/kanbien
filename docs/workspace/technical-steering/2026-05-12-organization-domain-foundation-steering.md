## Product Handoff

- Product Discovery status: `ready-for-technical-steering`
- Product intent preserved: yes. The Organization domain foundation must let root admins and tenant admins manage the full tenant-scoped organization structure, including organizations, legal details, locations, weekly opening-hour slots, opening-hour exceptions, business units, memberships, public logos, system catalogues, separated-by-type search, and private background exports.
- Product questions resolved or carried as blockers:
  - root admins and tenant admins both manage organization-domain data from v1.
  - tenant admins manage all tenant-owned organization-domain records in their current customer/account space.
  - root admins share the general experience but see broader functionality/entities and own system catalogue management.
  - organizations are manually created by admins; no default organization is auto-created.
  - organizations and business units both support parent/child hierarchy with max depth 10 and cycle prevention.
  - parent archive requires either archive-whole-branch or move children elsewhere.
  - one active legal profile per organization in v1, with optional tax/VAT number and optional registered address.
  - locations are many per organization; head-office flags are descriptive booleans, not uniqueness constraints; geocoordinates are optional.
  - weekly opening hours are weekday-specific slots; a day may have multiple ordered slots, no active slot means closed, and overlapping slots are denied.
  - opening-hour exceptions can supersede normal weekly slots for closed days, closed time slots, special opening slots, or replacement day schedules.
  - memberships require real existing individual users or real existing business units; placeholders are not allowed.
  - v1 membership roles are fixed Organization-domain participation labels: owner, manager, member, and viewer; they are not platform authz grants.
  - integration records are deferred from v1 implementation; if revived later they must stay high level and exclude secrets, endpoints, payload samples, credentials, and provider configuration.
  - branding v1 includes one primary public logo type with automatic public delivery after accepted upload; public delivery is approved for S-012 task breakdown by the completed public logo technical signoff gate.
  - logo replacement updates public usage everywhere after the replacement asset is accepted as safe/usable, using an app-controlled asset seam rather than raw bucket URLs.
  - reference catalogues are system-owned; tenant admins can use values; root admins can create/edit/archive/deprecate/replace values.
  - broad search spans all pieces and returns results separated by type.
  - exports are section-selectable, background-job-backed, private, requester-personal, PIN/password protected ZIP bundles containing JSON plus selected actual files, with actor choice between current-only and include-retained data, and expire after 24 hours or manual deletion.
  - export jobs support ready/failed notifications, cancel while pending/running, retry with previous or changed options, and download PIN view while available.
  - audit/change history is behind the scenes for v1.
  - import/bulk upload, recurring holiday calendars, seasonal/external opening-hour feeds, deep integration configuration, integration export, CSV export, request-time export snapshots, generated placeholder export image files, multiple active legal profiles, public non-logo organization pages, and admin-visible audit UI are out of v1.
- New family or template decision: `approved-new-family`
