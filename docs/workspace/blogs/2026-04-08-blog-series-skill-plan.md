# Blog Series Skill Plan

## Goal

Support a repeatable way to turn lived repo history and your personal journey
into thoughtful blog posts without flattening everything into generic startup
or AI content.

## Proposed Skills

### 1. Blog Series Strategist

#### Purpose

- shape the series arc
- choose post order
- prevent overlap between posts
- connect repo evolution to narrative themes

#### Inputs

- commit history
- architecture/docs evolution
- your high-level thesis

#### Outputs

- series overview
- post titles
- distinct core thesis for each post

### 2. Founder Interviewer

#### Purpose

- ask targeted reflective questions
- extract your real experiences, turning points, and language
- avoid generic “AI is changing everything” filler

#### Inputs

- one post topic at a time
- previous notes and your answers

#### Outputs

- interview notes
- candidate anecdotes
- pull quotes
- tensions and contradictions worth keeping

### 3. Story Distiller

#### Purpose

- turn raw answers into a clear narrative shape
- separate insight from chronology
- identify the strongest through-line for a post

#### Inputs

- interview answers
- repo references
- series plan

#### Outputs

- recommended angle
- suggested structure
- opening and closing options

### 4. Technical Grounding Editor

#### Purpose

- make sure each post is anchored in real repo work
- avoid overstating what the platform or process actually does
- point to concrete examples when needed

#### Inputs

- draft post
- repo docs and history

#### Outputs

- accuracy notes
- missing examples
- suggested technical anchors

### 5. Voice And Publication Editor

#### Purpose

- preserve a personal, credible tone
- keep the writing thoughtful rather than over-produced
- shape for blog readability

#### Inputs

- distilled draft
- your preferred voice

#### Outputs

- polished draft
- stronger transitions
- headline and subtitle options

## Practical Workflow

1. use `Blog Series Strategist` once for the overall series
2. use `Founder Interviewer` before each post draft
3. use `Story Distiller` to choose the strongest shape
4. use `Technical Grounding Editor` before finalizing
5. use `Voice And Publication Editor` for the final pass

## Suggested Question Cadence

Keep the sessions short and focused.

- 5 to 8 questions per post
- one main tension per session
- prefer concrete moments over abstract opinions
- ask for one real example whenever a claim feels broad

## Guardrails For The Writing Process

- do not let the series become generic AI boosterism
- keep mistakes and course-corrections visible
- prefer grounded technical examples over slogans
- preserve uncertainty where it is real
- use the repo as evidence, not as the whole story
