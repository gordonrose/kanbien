Contract Highlights

What This Pattern Governs

Keep record scanning and selection consistent across list-style page families even when the surrounding layout changes.

Anatomy

Compact summary first

The card carries title, subtitle, summary, tags, and selected-state feedback in one full-width button card.

Interaction

Selection without inline expansion

The pattern exists to trigger parent-owned detail reveal; inline expanded detail and card-local drawers are explicitly out of scope.

Reuse boundary

Pattern plus seam

The component seam captures the implementation boundary, while this pattern page keeps the broader summary-card UX contract visible in the route hierarchy.
