Contract Highlights

What This Pattern Governs

Keep the current page visible, reduce gracefully under width pressure, and never invent fake hierarchy just to complete the visual trail.

States

Full, collapsed, compact

The approved trail variants are a full trail, a collapsed middle segment, and a compact signpost menu when the row gets tight.

Content rule

Real hierarchy only

Parent steps should come from real page structure; filler labels and invented intermediate steps are explicitly out of bounds.

Pressure response

Reduce before overflow

Long labels must yield through collapse and compact behavior before they overlap the rest of the shared row chrome.
