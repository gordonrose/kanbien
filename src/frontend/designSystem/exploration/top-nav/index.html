Review Notes

What to look for

  • Brand mark keeps its proportions.
  • No overlap between brand, nav, and utilities.
  • `More` appears before crowding.
  • No `1 item + More` desktop state.
  • Menus layer above the row below.
  • Long labels truncate cleanly and still expose the full value via tooltip.
